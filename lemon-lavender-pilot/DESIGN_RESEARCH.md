# Guided training redesign — sources and adaptations

Official documentation reviewed in this work or the supplied parent research brief. This is documentation research, not a hands-on evaluation or ranking of paid platforms.

| Source | Pattern adapted for this local pilot |
| --- | --- |
| [Trainual: Training Paths](https://help.trainual.com/en/training-paths) | An ordered journey and an obvious next lesson. Our single Continue action resumes saved work; content remains accessible from the outline. |
| [Seismic Learning: Recorded Practice Questions](https://intercom.help/lessonly/en/articles/2667286-recorded-practice-questions) | Bring rehearsal into training and retain an artifact for coaching. This pilot adds audio only, with explicit recording controls and private local replay. |
| [Seismic Learning: Feedback Criteria](https://intercom.help/lessonly/en/articles/5595729-feedback-criteria) | Make feedback concrete and observable. We separate correction, what to continue, and a short retry task; no numerical readiness threshold is invented. |
| [Yoodli: Build and Customize Roleplays](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/11565137-how-to-build-and-customize-roleplays) | Present scenario context, customer goals and desired behaviors before practice. Our current actor remains a clearly labeled scripted fixture. |
| [Yoodli: Create a Program](https://support.yoodli.ai/en/articles/11824812-how-to-create-a-program) | Sequence learning and practice into a program. Difficulty labels here are recommendations, not automatically enforced passing levels. |
| [TalentLMS: Assignments](https://help.talentlms.com/hc/en-us/articles/9651387967004-How-to-work-with-assignments-in-TalentLMS) | Keep submitted work and instructor review distinct from content completion. Manager review remains necessary for retry/readiness. |

## Readiness-kit adaptation

The parent provided a review of all six pages and visuals of Cleaning Business Fundamentals, **Before You Hand Them the Phone: Sales Rep Readiness & Coaching Kit**, Library file `libfile_979975bddaa88191bf5e32c8e153b3ac` (`Sales_Rep_Readiness_Coaching_Kit_CBF.pdf`). This implementation uses that reviewed brief; it does not claim a second full PDF review here.

- Pages 2–3: five manager-assessed areas—company knowledge, conversation skills, practical application, setup/follow-through, performance ownership. Each starts Not assessed. Demonstrated / Needs practice requires identified evidence. Every submitted checklist is a retained snapshot; repeats with identical evidence do not duplicate it.
- Pages 3, 5–6: a single observable focus, one thing to continue, one behavior to improve and a short retry. Policy violations remain a separate correction barrier in call review.
- Page 4: foundations → supported practice → manager-approved customer work as a conceptual progression. No date/week or lesson completion grants live-call access.
- Page 3: whole-job handoff taught through priorities, recommendation, expectations, unresolved questions and next action. The practice CRM stays generic while the Jobber schema is blocked.
- Page 5: sale/non-sale/uncertain outcomes are useful review examples; closing every call is not the objective when a fit or policy problem requires escalation.
- Pages 5–6: the manager's focus is attached to new attempts. A modest first-ten-attempt view links actual transcripts/recordings and manual observed/not-yet/not-assessable judgments. After ten, the manager chooses whether to keep/change the focus; no passing percentage is invented.

## Original content coverage

7 lessons × 3 short steps, approximately 46 minutes at the stated reading/practice estimates. Every step contains an objective, reason, three practical instructions, original fictional example, common mistake, exception/escalation boundary, and an explained decision check. Selected steps save original learner writing for human review.

Beginner examples: price-first inquiry, choosing a service, initial deep, separating first/recurring price. Intermediate: ask what an objection means, acknowledge interruptions, handle one of several concerns at a time. Advanced: quarterly non-fit, pressure to waive a deep, >30-day reset, over-4,000-square-foot walkthrough, complaint without unsupported remedy, inside-48-hour cancellation with unapproved fee. The guided full call and follow-up simulator use actual saved actions for provisional evidence.

Current company decisions always outrank generic mentorship. We do not derive prices, fees, discounts, guarantees, service scope, pass criteria or employment outcomes from these sources. Full mentorship inventory review remains incomplete.
