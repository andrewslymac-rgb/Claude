# Owner review decisions

These are proposed rubric weights, not an approved score or passing rule. No numerical grade, automatic readiness threshold or employment decision is implemented. Manager review remains required.

| Category | Proposed weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Greeting and professionalism | 5% |
| Structured discovery | 18% |
| Listening and relevant value | 10% |
| Objection handling | 15% |
| Closing and accurate handoff | 30% |
| Scope and pricing accuracy | 10% |
| Call structure and control | 7% |
| Spoken clarity and pacing | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Version: `ll-sales-rubric-draft-v1`. Closing is the largest proposed category. Spoken clarity/pacing requires actual listening evidence; it cannot be inferred from transcript timestamps, network waits, accent, personality or health. A qualified service request is not a booking/payment. A truthful escalation or non-fit can be the appropriate result. False policy, invented discounts and waived mandatory deep cleaning remain blockers requiring correction/review.

Decisions still needed:

1. Approve or revise these weights and define what evidence a manager needs for readiness. There is no proposed automatic pass threshold to accept accidentally.
2. Confirm payment/deposit rules, discount authority, cancellation fee/waiver authority and named escalation contacts.
3. Confirm window and freezer units and how often recurring extras are charged/performed.
4. Confirm what staff may promise about screening, insurance/bonding, satisfaction guarantees, products/supplies, customer presence/access, contract terms, service area, availability and specialized turnover work. Until then, use manager clarification.
5. For follow-up, settle holiday exclusions and final-message delivery channel(s). The existing owner-approved five-minute response / business-day sequence, Pacific business hours, response/opt-out stops, 48-hour cancellation notice and deep reset after more than 30 days are retained; these are not being reopened as unknowns.

The current approved pricing, mandatory initial deep and service-request-versus-booking distinction remain authoritative. Generic mentorship content cannot override them.
