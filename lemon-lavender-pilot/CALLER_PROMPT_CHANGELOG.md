## Adding or refining content

1. Start from current owner-approved company truth. Unknown service, payment, insurance, screening, guarantee, supply or availability details stay unresolved. Mentorship material cannot authorize policy.
2. Extend the existing content dictionaries and typed/allowlisted builder. Define customer facts, motivation, communication behavior, hidden concern, buying signals, decision boundary and win/loss criteria. Keep all data fictional.
3. Keep the customer actor, action/outcome validator and evidence feedback separate. A customer yes never substitutes for an actual practice action or confirmed operational event.
4. Bump relevant versions; never mutate a saved attempt to fit newer content. Add branch tests showing resistance before requirements and the right agreement/refusal afterward. Verify old snapshots still replay/review correctly.
5. Regenerate review material, inspect the trainer prompt preview, and test locally without a provider. A live acceptance test needs a separately bounded authorization; no retry may erase or reset prior spend history.
