## Delivered behavior

- **Interrupt and recover locally.** “Let me speak” immediately stops current/queued customer sources, records canceled/suppressed chunks, and holds subsequent playback. “Let customer respond” explicitly recovers with a new application generation after a 350 ms output-signal quiet interval. Duplicate delivery does not replay audio; conflicting and out-of-order delivery closes the flow. Controls are bounded, CSRF protected and limited to the owning learner. Recording and billable connection time continue while held.
- **Supported protocol only.** The relay sends bounded application-authored `session.instructions.append` messages through its transport thread. It does not invent a provider cancellation or output-done event. The provider acknowledgment never releases playback. This is a manual playback control, not proven automatic speech interruption. Discarded speech may remain in model context; late model speech after a quiet interval still needs live acceptance. [OpenAI playback-control guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/voice-server-controls?api=live).
- **Record actual rendered audio.** The existing two-input AudioWorklet now captures silence continuously and returns its actual flushed end frame. Final metadata requires contiguous accepted chunks and exact frame/end agreement. Missing, unflushed or mismatched evidence is not offered as a complete conversation. Original speaker WAVs remain intact. Scheduled/canceled frame reports are diagnostic; the saved render graph is the replay source. Hardware output latency and microphone echo remain unmeasured.
- **Transcript seeking backed by listening.** Trainer/admin can mark an interval on a complete rendered WAV, choose confidence, confirm listening and save an append-only annotation tied to that WAV’s SHA-256. Transcript buttons seek to those intervals and highlight the corresponding entries. Corrections preserve earlier versions; exports include annotation history without reviewer account data. Approximate provider caption times never create automatic anchors. Older calls keep separate tracks and cannot receive invented combined alignment. [OpenAI transcript/playback distinction](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/live-conversations).
- **Actual live preparation uses selected callers.** `/course/1/live/` previews all 13 personas and three difficulties for a full conversation. Start builds the saved versioned scenario, actual caller prompt, draft rubric, matching CRM fields and scenario-specific transcript-coaching instructions. The controller independently checks the selected pair before reserving anything. Old launchers authorize no new pairs; changing a selector cannot widen a grant. All 39 pairs were checked offline, including manual review and non-fit pricing. Only fictional spoken transcript and fixed server-authored fictional instructions enter the coaching payload.
- **A queue-gap fix.** New audio keeps the existing contiguous schedule when at least one render quantum remains; the 30 ms startup/recovery margin is no longer reapplied unnecessarily to every late packet. The 100 ms polling interval, 24 kHz PCM format, `gpt-live-1` and `marin` voice remain the inspected settings. No voice/model switch was made. This fixes a reproducible scheduling case, not a demonstrated cause of robotic sound.

The customer remains separate from hints, deterministic outcome checks and coaching. Text coaching explicitly warns that captions can include interrupted/unplayed customer speech. Live consent, note completeness and readiness still require manager review. No numerical grade or automatic readiness threshold was introduced.
