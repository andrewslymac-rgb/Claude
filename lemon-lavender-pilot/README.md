## Walk through it

1. Sign in as `learner@example.invalid`. Open the local email preview and confirm. This sends no email; the link is browser-bound, single-use, and expires after ten minutes. GET/scanner requests cannot consume it.
2. Use **Continue**. You may open the outline and review any lesson. **Save for later** preserves an unfinished written answer. Knowledge checks teach facts; they are not competence scores.
3. Open **Practice**. The guided call is available after the welcome and conversation lessons (earlier valid credit still counts). Play the customer, then select discovery, relevant value, pricing, and objection responses. Optional recording is a separate expandable panel.
4. Save practice notes for fictional Taylor: 2,000 square feet, biweekly, $570 required initial deep, $220 subsequent biweekly visits, customer priorities, unresolved questions, and next step. Then explicitly request the next step. Customer agreement depends on these actual practice actions and an accurate saved quote.
5. Submit for provisional feedback. Transcript evidence distinguishes a service request from a booking, payment, or operational quote approval.
6. Sign in as `trainer@example.invalid`. Review the attempt and any recordings, record one thing to continue, one behavior to improve, and a short retry task. Known policy violations cannot receive readiness approval. The learner can retry after this review.
7. Open the learner's **Coaching & readiness** page to choose the next ten-attempt focus and enter evidence for the five readiness areas. Earlier focuses and readiness snapshots are retained. A legitimate non-fit/escalation may be a valid outcome.
8. `admin@example.invalid` can also open **Usage & costs**. Changing a target or limit authorizes no spending.

## Boundaries and data

SQLite and recordings are isolated under `.data`. Recordings are random-ID files with mode 0600 inside a 0700 directory, served through authenticated object-level checks, never static URLs. Canonical mono PCM WAV parsing validates size, duration, encoding and lengths. Duplicate uploads are idempotent; closed/expired attempts cannot add new recordings. Unsaved audio exists only in browser memory and is discarded on leaving. The browser releases its microphone on Stop or page exit. Only learner attempt pages permit microphone access; camera and display capture remain prohibited everywhere.

Default practice limits: ten inbound/follow-up starts per UTC day, twenty minutes per call, twenty-four selected responses, one active attempt per assignment. Current provider spend limit is zero. There is no automatic employment, accent, personality, health, or audio cadence assessment.

Demo role access is intentionally available to anyone using this Mac; this is not production identity protection. No owner password, usable demo password, superuser, or persistent owner credential exists. CSRF, no-store responses, loopback-only checks, strict same-site cookies, escaped input and same-origin asset/network policy remain enforced.

Existing policy/scenario/rubric/limit snapshots and audio are preserved per attempt. New calls use `samantha-v3`: shorter original wording, individually paced phrase groups and explicit pauses using the already-installed Samantha voice. Legacy/v2 files are unchanged. This remains synthetic prerecorded speech; perceived naturalness needs listening review. See `AUDIO_PREVIEW_NOTES.md`.
