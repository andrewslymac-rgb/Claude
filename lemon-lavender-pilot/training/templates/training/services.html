What we offer

We clean homes. Our current options are:

  • Deluxe Deep: a one-time service or the required first visit before recurring cleaning.
  • Recurring cleaning: weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. We do not offer quarterly service.
  • Move In / Out: cleaning for a move, with the four extras listed below included.

After more than 30 days without a clean, another initial deep is required. There is no waiver for the required deep clean.

How the practice estimate is calculated

The production rate is $85 per hour. Approved square-feet-per-hour rates: weekly 850, biweekly 775, monthly 650, deep 300, and move 275. The base is the larger of the minimum or 5 × ceiling(square feet × 17 ÷ production rate). The final total rounds up to the next $5.

Promises to get right

  • A request is not a booking. The team must confirm availability; customers cannot book online right now.
  • Cancellation notice is 48 hours. Ask a manager about fees or exceptions.
  • No discretionary discounts. Say “I’m sorry, we don’t have any discounts available right now.” Offer customer-mentioned active promotions only under verified dates, eligibility and terms; do not invent or stack offers. Payment/deposit terms, VA authority and specific guarantees still need confirmation.
  • Business hours are Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM Pacific. After-hours first outreach is due by 8:05 AM at the next business opening. Holiday handling is still pending.
