What the exercise checks

  • First outreach within five simulated minutes during business hours, or by 8:05 AM Pacific at the next business opening for after-hours leads.
  • The exact Monday–Friday no-response sequence, including Friday → Monday → Tuesday rollover.
  • Valid email and explicit channel preferences.
  • Immediate exit after a response and full stop after an opt-out.
  • A recorded callback promise instead of a blind sequence restart.

The final message is rehearsed without choosing its unresolved delivery channels. There is no numeric grade or automatic pass.
