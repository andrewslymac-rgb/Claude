GOOGLE & FACEBOOK LEADS · FOLLOW-UP GUIDE

Follow up promptly.
Know when to stop.

Give a new lead a clear opportunity to connect. Once they respond, handle the conversation and the promise you make.

The first five minutes

Answer live inbound calls immediately. During business hours, for a missed call or new inbound lead, call within five minutes. If unanswered, leave a voicemail, text, and email when a valid email exists. Complete that first outreach within five minutes, honoring explicit channel preferences.

A valid email is needed for every email step. Skip email when it is invalid or missing; do not invent an address. Respect a stated channel restriction even if the usual sequence includes that channel.

If there is zero response

Follow-up days are business days, Monday through Friday. A fictional Friday day 1 rolls to Monday day 2 and Tuesday day 3. Saturday and Sunday are skipped between follow-up days. Company hours are Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM Pacific (America/Los_Angeles). Pacific time changes between PST and PDT; use the named time zone instead of a fixed UTC offset.

After-hours leads: first outreach by 8:05 AM

Complete first outreach by 8:05 AM Pacific on the next business day. Use Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM in America/Los_Angeles. A Friday evening or weekend lead has a Monday 8:05 AM deadline under the regular weekday rule. The first outreach day becomes day 1 of the no-response sequence.

The exercise uses the next business opening: a weekday lead before 8 AM uses that morning; a lead at or after 5 PM uses the next weekday. Call, leave voicemail if unanswered, text, and use valid email, honoring preferences. Live inbound calls still require immediate answering. Pacific daylight-saving changes are applied to the deadline date.

Fixed fictional dates demonstrate the rule. Holiday treatment remains unconfirmed; this is not a holiday calendar or a real schedule.

“Morning” and “end of day” are labels, not invented clock times. Later contact windows use Monday–Friday weekday labels. The after-hours example calculates a fictional first-outreach deadline only; no outreach is scheduled. Holiday exclusions are not established. Do not assume the final message is text plus email.

A response changes the task

Any customer response exits the no-response sequence. Answer the question, continue discovery, or record the agreed next step. A reply that asks for email only is a response and a preference to honor.

An opt-out stops all outreach. It also stops a previously tracked callback. Do not continue merely because a reminder or standard sequence exists.

Keep the callback promise

If the customer says “call me Tuesday,” clarify the date, time, time zone, and preferred channel. Record the specific promise and any missing detail. Do not keep sending the no-response sequence while waiting for that callback. The exercise stores a note for human handling; it creates no reminder, appointment, message, or real lead update.
