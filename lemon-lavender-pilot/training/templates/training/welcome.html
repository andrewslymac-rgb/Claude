Our values and mission

Our mission is to become Portland’s most trusted cleaning company for people’s homes. Andrew describes background-checked, fully trained cleaning technicians and high-quality, thorough top-to-bottom cleaning.

Our handbook values are Integrity, Quality, Professionalism, Safety, and Reliability. Use accurate information, follow approved scope, communicate professionally, protect safety, and follow through on commitments.

Our services at a glance

Residential only. We care for people’s homes. We do not offer quarterly service.

  • Move In / Out cleaning.
  • Deluxe Deep as a standalone service or the initial visit.
  • Recurring weekly, biweekly, or monthly cleaning. A new recurring plan requires an initial deep clean; there is no waiver. After more than 30 days without a clean, another initial deep clean is required.

Cancellation notice is 48 hours. Fee amounts and waiver authority remain unresolved; escalate questions instead of inventing them.

Customers can request service. There is currently no customer online booking. A requested date must not be described as a confirmed booking.

Working with Portland from the Philippines

PHILIPPINES · ASIA/MANILACheck Philippine time for the specific date

Portland is in Oregon, in the Pacific Northwest of the United States. The local clock changes with daylight saving time. The Philippines does not use that seasonal change, so the difference is not the same all year. Use the date and named time zone when arranging a call.

Home area is quoted in square feet (sq ft), and these estimates are in US dollars ($). Biweekly means every two weeks here, not twice a week. Confirm the customer’s meaning.

A US address commonly includes a street number, street name, unit if any, city, state, and ZIP code. Ask the customer to confirm unclear details. This practice uses no real addresses, alarm codes, entry instructions, or payment details.

The regional places named in the VA SOP include Portland, Happy Valley, Oregon City, Milwaukie, Clackamas, Lake Oswego, West Linn, and Gresham. This is geography orientation; confirm current service-area coverage before promising service. Residential-only handling is confirmed by Andrew.

Portland commonly has wetter cool months and drier summers. Seasonal routines can affect cleaning needs and scheduling. Ask about actual mud, tracked-in debris, buildup, moving dates, or timing concerns; do not assume a home’s condition or access from its location.

Cleaning-industry fundamentals

A first deep clean and a recurring maintenance visit have different purposes. Discover the condition, priorities, size, requested scope, and timing before discussing the estimate. Never promise a specific outcome, product safety, or an exception that has not been approved.

Possible practice callers include a resident maintaining a routine, someone moving, or someone seeking a one-time deep clean. Each caller is different. Confirm each caller’s needs and decision process.

Keep expectations concrete: what service is requested, what remains uncertain, what price is estimated, and who will confirm the next step. Respond thoughtfully to complaints, record facts, and ask a manager when authority is unclear.
