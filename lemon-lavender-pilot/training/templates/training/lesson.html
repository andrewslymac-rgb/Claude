CALL GUIDE · ABOUT 5 MINUTES

Discover before
you quote.

Help the customer choose the right service and agree on what happens next.

1. Understand the need

Ask about home size, current condition, priorities, frequency, and timing. Repeat the important details back. Price should follow discovery.

“What would make this cleaning feel like a success for you?”

2. Recommend a service and explain the estimate

Connect the scope to the customer’s stated needs. For our fictional 2,000 sq ft home:

ServicePractice base estimate
Monthly$265
Biweekly$220
Weekly$200
Deluxe Deep$570
Move In / Out$620

We serve residential customers only: weekly, biweekly, or monthly recurring service, not quarterly. Every recurring plan begins with a required initial deep. After more than 30 days without a clean, another initial deep is required. Homes above 4,000 sq ft need a manual walkthrough. Do not invent a waiver or discount.

Practice extras: oven $45, fridge $45, dishwasher $35, cabinets $65. These four are included in Move In / Out. Windows are listed at $8 but the unit is unresolved. Window/freezer units and recurring extras frequency require clarification.

3. Address the concern, then ask

Listen for the actual objection. Explain relevant value and check whether it answers the concern. Ask for an explicit next step tied to the correct scope and estimate.

“Would you like me to record your request for the initial deep and biweekly plan, then have the team confirm availability?”

A customer’s “yes” is not quote approval, a paid deposit, or a confirmed booking. Cancellation notice is 48 hours. Cancellation fee amounts, waiver authority, payment, deposits, discount authority, holiday exclusions, final-message channels, and escalation contacts still need approval. The separate lead follow-up lesson covers the owner-approved sequence.

4. Record, review, and improve

Save needs, quote figures, and follow-up notes. A legitimate non-fit or escalation can be the right outcome. Review wins, losses, and uncertain calls with a manager, then coach one observable behavior over the next 10 calls.

The demo saves the responses you choose so your trainer can review them. Scoring weights and passing rules still need approval. Accent, personality, and health are not scored.
